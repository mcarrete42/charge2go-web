Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

What types of venue is charge2go power bank rental suitable for?

charge2go power bank rental works in almost any venue with plenty of foot traffic: bars and clubs, restaurants and cafés, hotels, gyms, hospitals and clinics, shopping centres, stations and airports, and events or festivals. We install the power bank rental station for free, with no commitment, and your business earns passive income from day one.

How much does it cost to install the machine in my venue?

Nothing. Installation, the machine and maintenance are completely free for the venue. charge2go covers all costs. All you need to provide is the space and a standard power socket.

How much space does the machine take up?

The station has a small footprint, similar to an office bin. It fits on any bar, counter or corner with customer traffic.

What does my staff need to do?

Nothing. Literally. The machine runs on its own. The customer chooses, pays contactless and picks up the power bank. If anything goes wrong, the charge2go team handles it directly with the customer.

How much money can my venue make?

It depends on your foot traffic. With an average flow of customers, the machine generates monthly passive income from day one. Get in touch and we'll give you the details with no obligation.

What happens if the machine breaks down?

The system is monitored remotely 24/7. charge2go spots issues before you do and handles the fix. Your business is not affected.

Do I need to install an app or any special system?

No. The machine only needs a standard 220V socket. No network setup, software or integration with your systems is required.

Can I remove the machine if I no longer want it?

Yes. No commitment and no penalty. We agree on exit terms transparently from the start.

Where can I rent a power bank in Madrid or Barcelona?

charge2go power bank rental stations are available in hospitality and leisure venues in Madrid and Barcelona, such as Sala Apolo. You can find the nearest one through our partner venues. More details (in Spanish): how to rent a power bank near you.

How much does it cost to rent a power bank?

Renting a power bank from charge2go is charged by usage time: from €2.50 per 30 minutes or from €3 per hour, depending on the venue. A refundable deposit is held when you pick it up and, when you return the power bank, the actual usage time is calculated and the difference is refunded. Contactless payment at the station, no app or sign-up needed. Full breakdown (in Spanish): how much it costs to rent a power bank in Spain.

What is a power bank rental station?

A power bank rental station is a device installed in a public venue that lets customers borrow a power bank (also called a portable charger or battery pack), charge their phone and return it to any station in the network. charge2go installs these stations free of charge in businesses with high foot traffic.

Is it better to rent a power bank or buy one?

It depends on how you use it. If you need an occasional charge when you're out, renting a power bank is cheaper and more practical: you only pay for the time you use it, and you don't have to charge it or carry it around. Buying one makes sense if you need it every day.
